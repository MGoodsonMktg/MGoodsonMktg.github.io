About Me

A marketer who leads with heart and backs it with data

I'm a strategic marketing and communications professional with more than fifteen years of experience building integrated campaigns, compelling brand narratives, and mission-driven community programs across nonprofit, entertainment, B2B, and DTC environments.

My career has been defined by a rare combination: I am both a gifted storyteller and a technical marketer, equally comfortable crafting a subject line that converts and presenting executive-level performance reports that drive strategy.

I have scaled email programs from the ground up, launched ambassador programs, managed social media for nonprofits and television productions, led PR for filmmaker premieres at Sundance, SXSW, and the Toronto International Film Festival, directed content operations across 27 websites, and built scholarship and educational programs for high school students. Whatever the industry, I bring the same conviction: the best marketing connects people to something that genuinely matters.

I am actively seeking roles where my skills serve a mission I believe in, including education, health equity, travel, arts and entertainment, and mission-driven consumer brands.
